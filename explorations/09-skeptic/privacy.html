“What happens to what I send you?”

Here's where everything you send us goes.

Two kinds of information reach Tooey: what you type into the contact form, and, if you join the pilot, your restaurant's sales. Each one is below, with who sees it and how to have it deleted.

The contact form

01“What does the contact form collect?”

Only what you type into it.

Your name, your restaurant, your email, and your message, plus your phone number and POS if you add them. FormSubmit, a form-delivery service, sends it on to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

02“What do you use it for?”

Writing back to you.

That's the only use. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for ads.

Your sales, if you join the pilot

03“If I join, what happens to my sales?”

They make your forecast, and only yours.

We take your item-level sales each night through the download we set up from your POS, and use them to forecast for your restaurant. We don't sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them.

04“Who can see them?”

The people on our team building your forecast.

Nobody else gets your data from us. FormSubmit carries contact-form messages to our inbox, and your POS company already holds your sales, as it did before Tooey.

05“What if I share recipes or inventory later?”

They stay with your restaurant.

If you ever share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records with us, we use them only to help with your own prep and purchasing.

Stopping and deleting

06“Can I have it deleted?”

Yes. Email us.

Write to tooeyteam@gmail.com and ask us to delete your messages, stop the nightly pull, or remove the sales we've stored. Any one of those, or all three.
