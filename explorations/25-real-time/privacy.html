Privacy

Two kinds of information reach us: what you send through the contact form, and, if you work with us, your restaurant's sales.

The contact form

The form sends your name, restaurant, email and anything else you fill in through FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use it only to reply to you.

Your restaurant's sales

Your sales are used only to work out counts and plans for your restaurant. We do not sell them, share them with other restaurants or publish them. Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting your information

Ask us to delete anything we hold about you or your restaurant and we will. Write to tooeyteam@gmail.com.
