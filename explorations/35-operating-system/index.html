What Tooey does

Every day, from your own sales, Tooey works out three things for you.

  1. Prep

    How much of each dish to make, ready every morning before prep: a little over what should sell.

  2. Shifts

    The right people on at the right time, matched to how busy each night will be.

  3. Growth

    Fuller slow nights, and regulars who come back.

Built for independent restaurants.

The big chains have whole teams doing this planning. An independent has the owner. Tooey does that planning for them.

  • One location or a few

    Sit-down restaurants, run by the people who own them.

  • Your own sales, nobody else's

    Each restaurant's counts come only from its own sales, never pooled with anyone else's.

Running in about a week.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3
