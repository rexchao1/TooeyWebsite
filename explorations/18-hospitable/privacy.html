Privacy

Short version: we use what you send us to reply to you, and a restaurant’s sales only to make that restaurant’s counts.

The contact form

When you send the form, your name, restaurant, email and anything else you type go through FormSubmit, a form service, to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use them only to reply to you. We don’t add you to a mailing list.

Your restaurant’s sales

During a pilot we set up a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. We use them only to make your restaurant’s counts.

  • They are not sold.
  • They are not shared with other restaurants.
  • They are not published.
  • Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting your data

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and we’ll delete what we hold about you or your restaurant.

Who we are

Tooey LLC, a small team of university students. Questions go to the same address.
