Privacy

What we collect, what it’s for, who sees it, and how to have it removed.

When you write to us

The contact form sends us your name, your restaurant, your email, and your message, plus your phone number and POS if you add them. It goes through FormSubmit, a form service, to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use it to reply to you. We don’t sell it, and we don’t use it for ads.

Your sales, if you join the pilot

We set up a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. Those sales are used only to forecast for your restaurant. We don’t sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them.

If you later share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records, they stay with your restaurant and are used only to help with its prep and purchasing.

Who sees it

Only the people on the Tooey team who build your forecast.

Having it removed

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and ask us to delete your messages, stop the nightly download, or remove the sales we’ve stored.
