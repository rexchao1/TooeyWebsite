Reading the list
Predicted
How many of the dish we expect you to sell today.
Prep
How many to make. A little over Predicted, so a rush doesn’t leave the line short.
vs. last Tue
Predicted against what sold on the same day last week, so a big swing stands out before service.
A blank
The dish has been on the menu under about five weeks, or sells under one a day. Instead of guessing, the list says why. A blank is not a zero.

If you know something about the day that isn’t in your sales, you know something the list doesn’t. The call stays yours.

Getting a list for your restaurant

You need about five weeks of past sales in your POS to start. From there, setup takes about a week.

  1. Write to us

    Tell us about your restaurant and which POS you use. The form is at the bottom of this page.

  2. We set up a nightly download from your POS

    It works with most systems. Square, Toast, and Clover sales exports are recognized, and other exports can be mapped.

  3. About a week of setup

    You tell us what matters in your kitchen and choose how you want the list set up. Then you check it before you rely on it.

  4. Your list, every morning

    It arrives by email before prep, and you can print it for the line. During the pilot you check the counts against what actually sold, then decide.

About Tooey

Tooey LLC is a small team of university students. We’re building software for independent and casual dine-in restaurants, especially ones where the owner has neither the time nor the staff to dig through their own numbers. What we want it to do is help you make more money while spending less time managing the place.

We started with prep because it’s a call your kitchen makes every morning, and your POS already holds what it takes to make it well. The rest is where we want to take it, and none of it is built yet. Today, Tooey makes the prep list.

Today

  • A prep count for each dish, every morning, from your own sales

Where we want to take it

  • Which dishes actually make money, and where costs creep in
  • Staff planned around how busy a day is likely to be
  • Forecasts that also weigh weather and local events
  • Bringing customers back, and bringing new ones in
