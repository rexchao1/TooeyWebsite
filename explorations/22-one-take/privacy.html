Privacy

The contact form

When you send the form, what you type goes through FormSubmit, a form service, to our inbox at tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use it only to reply to you.

Your restaurant’s sales

If you join, Tooey reads your restaurant’s sales to work out your counts. They are used only for your restaurant. We don’t sell them, share them with other restaurants or publish them. Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting it

Ask at tooeyteam@gmail.com and we’ll delete what you sent us, and your restaurant’s sales.
