Privacy

Two kinds of information reach us: what you send through the contact form, and, if your restaurant joins, its sales.

The contact form

The form is delivered by FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use what you write only to reply to you.

Your restaurant's sales

Your sales are used only to work out your own restaurant's counts. We do not sell them, share them with other restaurants or publish them. Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting your information

Write to tooeyteam@gmail.com and we will delete what we hold about you or your restaurant.
