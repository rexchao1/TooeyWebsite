What happens to what you send us.

  1. The contact form goes through FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use what you write only to reply to you.
  2. If you join the pilot, your restaurant's sales are used only for your restaurant.
  3. We don't sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them. Only the Tooey team sees them.
  4. Want something deleted? Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and we'll delete it.
