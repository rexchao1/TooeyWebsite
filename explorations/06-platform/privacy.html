Privacy

What we collect, what it’s for, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

If you write to us

The form on this site sends your name, your restaurant, your email, and your message, plus your phone number and POS if you add them. It travels through a service called FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use it to reply to you. We don’t sell it, and we don’t use it for ads.

If you join the pilot

We set up a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. Those sales are used to forecast for your restaurant and for nothing else.

We don’t sell them, we don’t share them with other restaurants, and we don’t publish them.

If you share more later

If you later share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records, they stay with your restaurant. We use them only to help with your prep and purchasing.

Who can see it

Only the people on the Tooey team who build your forecast. FormSubmit carries the contact form to our inbox.

Having it removed

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com. You can ask us to delete your messages, stop the nightly download, or remove the sales we’ve stored. Ask for any of it, or all of it.

Nothing on this page is in pencil. It describes how things work today.
