A blank means the dish has been on the menu under about five weeks, or sells under about one a day. We leave it blank and say why rather than guess. A blank is a decline, not a zero.

How it arrives
By email, each morning before prep. Print it and keep it on the line.
What it’s made from
Your own item-level sales, the day of the week, the time of year, and holidays. Every count is redone each night with that day’s sales.
What it doesn’t use yet
Weather, local events, and reservations. They’re in the plan, not in the count.
Where the sales come from
Your POS. We set up a nightly download that works with most systems. Square, Toast, and Clover sales exports are recognized, and other exports can be mapped.
What you need to start
About five weeks of past sales.
Setup
About a week. You tell us what matters in your kitchen, choose how the list is set up for you, and check it before you rely on it.
Who decides
You do. While we’re in the pilot, you hold our counts up against what actually sold, then decide whether to prep from them.

Why prep came first

Most of the calls you make about a week lean on one question: how much are we going to sell?

It decides how much to prep, what to order, how many people to put on, and whether a slow Tuesday needs a push. So the first thing we built is an answer to it: a forecast of tomorrow’s sales, dish by dish. Prep is the first thing standing on it. Ordering, staffing, and marketing would stand on the same footing.

There was a plainer reason too. A prep count can be checked against what sold, every single day. We would rather earn your trust on a number you can check by close than ask for it on one you can’t.

Where Tooey is going

The aim is one place where an independent restaurant can see how it’s doing and decide what to do next, built from records you already keep, starting with your POS. This is the direction as we see it now. It will change as we learn from the kitchens using it.

Each piece is meant to grow the same way. It shows you something first, then suggests what to do about it. Only where you’ve said yes does it do the job for you.
