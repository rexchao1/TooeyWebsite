Privacy

What we collect, what we use it for, and who gets to see it. If anything here is unclear, write to us and ask.

If you write to us

The contact form sends us your name, your restaurant, your email, your phone number and POS if you add them, and your message. It goes through a service called FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use it to reply to you. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for ads.

If you join the pilot

We set up a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. Your sales are used for one thing: forecasting for your restaurant.

We don't sell them, we don't share them with other restaurants, and we don't publish them.

If you share more later

If you later share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records, they stay with your restaurant. We use them only to help with your prep and purchasing.

Who sees it

Only the people on the Tooey team who build your forecast.

Taking it back

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and ask. We'll delete your messages, stop the nightly download, or remove the sales we've stored.

This website

This site has no analytics and no ad trackers. Its fonts are served from here, not from a font service.
