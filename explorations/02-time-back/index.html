Tooey does its work while your restaurant is closed.

After you lock up, it goes through the day's sales and works out how much of each dish to prep the next morning. Nobody types anything in, and there's nothing for you to keep running.

What happens between closing and prep

None of it needs you, or anyone on your staff, to be there.

  1. After close

    The day's sales come in from your POS.

    A nightly download brings in what sold that day, dish by dish. It works with most POS systems, and we set it up for you when you join.

  2. Overnight

    Tomorrow's counts are worked out again, dish by dish.

    Tooey looks at how each dish has sold in your restaurant before: on the same day of the week, at this time of year, and around holidays. From that it works out how many you're likely to sell tomorrow, and how many to make.

    It doesn't use weather, local events, or reservations yet.

  1. Before prep

    The list is waiting in your email before prep.

    Read it on your phone, or print it and put it up where the line can see it.

  1. 1

    Predicted is how many of that dish Tooey expects you to sell.

  2. 2

    Prep is how many to make. It runs a little over Predicted, so a rush doesn't leave the line short.

  3. 3

    vs. last Tue is the change from the same day last week. A big jump is worth a second look.

  4. 4

    A blank means Tooey declined to guess. Dishes on the menu less than about five weeks, or selling under about one a day, get the reason instead of a number. A blank is not a zero.

Everything Tooey needs from you

You're already the buyer, the scheduler, the bookkeeper, and the one who locks up. Tooey shouldn't turn into another thing to look after, so this is the whole list.

Time cardYour side

Before you start
About five weeks of past sales. They're already in your POS. Tooey needs that much history before it can make a first count.
The first week
About a week of setup with us. You tell us what matters in your kitchen and choose how your list is set up. Then you check it before you rely on it.
Each morning
Read the list. On your phone, or printed for the line.
During the pilot
Check the counts against what sold. That's how you find out whether the numbers hold up in your kitchen. Then you decide whether to keep Tooey.
Not your job
Exports, uploads, typing in sales. The download runs by itself each night, so there's no report to pull and no file to send.
