Privacy

Tooey LLC is a small team of university students. This page says what we do with what you send us.

The contact form

When you send the form, your name, restaurant, email and anything else you add go through FormSubmit, a form service, to our inbox at tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use them only to reply to you. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Your restaurant's sales

  • We use your sales only to work out counts and plans for your restaurant.
  • We do not sell them, share them with other restaurants or publish them.
  • Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting your data

Write to tooeyteam@gmail.com and we will delete your form message, your sales, or both.
