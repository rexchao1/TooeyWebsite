Privacy

The contact form

When you send the form, what you type goes through FormSubmit, a form delivery service, to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use it only to reply to you.

Your restaurant's sales

A restaurant's sales are used only to work out that restaurant's counts. They are not sold, not shared with other restaurants and not published. Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting your data

Ask us to delete your messages or your restaurant's sales and we will. Write to tooeyteam@gmail.com.
