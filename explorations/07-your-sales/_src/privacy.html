Privacy

What we collect, what we use it for, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

When you write to us

The form on this site sends us your name, your restaurant, your email, your phone number and POS if you add them, and your message. It goes through FormSubmit, a form service, to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use it to reply to you. We don’t sell it, and we don’t use it for ads.

If your restaurant joins the pilot

We set up a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. We use those sales to make prep counts for your restaurant and for nothing else. We don’t sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them.

If you later share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records, they stay with your restaurant too. We use them only to help with your prep and purchasing.

Who can see it

Only the people on the Tooey team who build your forecast. Messages from the contact form pass through FormSubmit on their way to our inbox.

Having it removed

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and tell us what you want gone. We’ll delete your messages, stop the nightly download, or remove the sales we’ve stored.

This website

No analytics, no ads, and no tracking cookies. Fonts and images load from this site, not from other companies’ servers.

Tooey LLC. Questions about any of this go to tooeyteam@gmail.com.
