Privacy

The form

What you send through the form goes to tooeyteam@gmail.com by way of FormSubmit, a form-to-email service. We use it only to reply to you.

Your sales

If your restaurant joins the pilot, its sales are used only to forecast for that restaurant. They are not sold, not shared with other restaurants, and not published. Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting it

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and we will delete what we hold about you or your restaurant.

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
