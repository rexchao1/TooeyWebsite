What happens to what you send us

Two kinds of information reach Tooey: what you type into the contact form, and, if your restaurant joins the pilot, its sales. Here is what each one is used for, who sees it, and how to have it removed.

The contact form

The form sends these fields, and nothing else:

  • Namerequired
  • Restaurantrequired
  • Emailrequired
  • Phoneoptional
  • Which POS you useoptional
  • Your messageoptional

FormSubmit, the form service we use, passes them to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use them to reply to you. We don’t sell them, and we don’t use them for ads.

Your sales, if you join the pilot

At setup we arrange a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. Those sales are used to forecast for your restaurant, and for nothing else.

We don’t sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them.

Recipes, inventory, and purchasing

If you later share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records, they stay with your restaurant. We use them only to help with your prep and purchasing.

Who sees it

The people on the Tooey team who build your forecast and answer your email.

FormSubmit carries contact messages to our inbox. Your POS company already holds your sales. We don’t send your data anywhere else.

Having it removed

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and ask us to delete your messages, stop the nightly download, or remove the sales we’ve stored. We will.
