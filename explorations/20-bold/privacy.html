Privacy

The contact form

When you send the form, FormSubmit passes your name, restaurant, email, and anything else you add to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use it only to reply to you.

Your sales

If you join the pilot, we set up a nightly download of your sales from your POS. Your sales are used only for your restaurant. We don't sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them.

Who sees it

Only the Tooey team.

Deleting it

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and ask. We will delete your message, your sales, or both.
