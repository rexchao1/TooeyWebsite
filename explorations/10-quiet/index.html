Prep is a little more than we expect you to sell.

Predicted is how many we think you’ll sell today. Prep is how many to make, with a little extra so a rush doesn’t leave the line short. The last number is the change from the same day last week, so a big swing stands out.

Every count starts from your own sales.

Tooey looks at what each dish has sold at your restaurant over past weeks, then allows for the calendar.

What goes in

  • Your past sales, dish by dish
  • The day of the week
  • The time of year
  • Holidays

Not yet

  • Weather
  • Local events
  • Reservations

When there isn’t enough to go on, we leave it blank.

A dish that’s been on the menu less than about five weeks, or sells less than about one a day, gets no number. The reason goes where the number would be. A blank means we declined to guess. It isn’t a zero.

Getting started takes about a week.

You’ll need about five weeks of past sales in your POS. We set up a nightly download from it, which works with most systems. During that week you tell us what matters in your kitchen, choose how your list is laid out, and check it before you rely on it.

From then on, print it in the morning and keep it on the line if that suits you.

We’re early, and we’d like you to check our work.

Tooey is a small team of university students, running a pilot with independent restaurants. While it runs, you hold each morning’s counts up against what you actually sold, and you decide what to make.

Prep is where we’re starting.

Over time we want Tooey to take on more of what an owner carries home at night: which dishes actually make money, where costs creep in, how many people a busy Friday needs, whether rain will keep Saturday quiet. The idea stays the same. The work happens while you’re closed, and the answer is there when you need it.
