Privacy

What we keep, why we keep it, and who sees it.

When you write to us

The contact form sends your name, your restaurant, your email, and your message, plus your phone number and POS if you add them. FormSubmit carries it to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use it only to reply to you. It is never sold or used for ads.

If your restaurant joins the pilot

Your sales are used to forecast for your restaurant and nothing else. They aren’t sold, shared with other restaurants, or published.

If you later share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records, they stay with your restaurant and are used only to help with your prep and purchasing.

Who sees it

Only the people on the Tooey team who build your forecast.

Having it removed

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and ask. We’ll delete your messages, stop the nightly download from your POS, or remove the sales we’ve stored.
