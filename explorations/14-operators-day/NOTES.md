# 14 · The owner's week

## Concept
One independent restaurant, seen from across the street, lives through a whole
week as you scroll: Monday 05:40 to Sunday lights-out, then Monday 05:40 again.
The page is the shopfront itself, painted aubergine, with the day signwritten on
the fascia, the dining room behind the big window, and the basement prep kitchen
in the light-well below. The voice is the owner's week, a little wry: "Nobody
sits down. Least of all you."

## Motion and how it's built
- **Load (about 2.8 s):** Monday 04:54 in the dark. The basement fluorescent
  catches with a flicker at 05:40, and the morning counts are written onto the
  masking-tape labels of the four tubs, one by one. The headline is lettered onto
  the dark glass at the same time.
- **Scroll (560vh section, sticky stage, about 4.6 screens of scrolling):** the
  scene is a pure function of time, `render(day, hour)`, driven by
  ScrollTrigger progress with a short GSAP ease for smoothing. Each day gets
  its own sky cycle, sun and moon arcs, façade light, door glass, and pendant
  glow.
  - Every diner in the window is one cover from an example week. As covers rise,
    each new diner walks in from the door side and sits. As covers fall they
    leave, last in, first out.
  - The tubs fill during morning prep and drain in step with that day's covers,
    leaving a sliver at close ("a little over").
  - Every dawn the light catches again and new counts are written.
  - Thursday afternoon, each counted tub gets a tick.
  - **The day name on the fascia is set in Imbue, and its weight follows the
    number of covers in the room, live.** Monday morning is hairline;
    Friday and Saturday nights go black.
- **Payoff:** after Sunday, the clock is back at Monday 05:40. The four tape
  labels peel off the tubs and fly to centre screen (FLIP from their SVG rects),
  becoming the legible count: "This part's real."
- **Week ruler:** seven buttons that jump to each day's key moment. The header
  CTA jumps straight to the form.
- **Build:** the SVG for both windows is generated by `_src/scene.mjs`
  (`node _src/build.mjs` writes `index.html` from `_src/index.template.html`).
  Runtime: `js/site.js`, plus vendored GSAP 3.12.5 and ScrollTrigger. There is
  no other library and no external request. Total folder size is about 320 KB.
- **Reduced motion:** the stage sits still at Monday 05:40. Below it is a
  storyboard of seven frames, cloned from the same SVG and drawn at each day's
  key moment, with the caption and the day name at that moment's weight. Monday's
  frame carries the tape labels.
- **No JS:** the façade shows the Monday 05:40 state (lit kitchen, counts on the
  tubs). The seven days follow as a plain list, and the form posts normally.

## Palette and type
- Aubergine `#3B2032`: shopfront paint and ground. It shifts darker and lighter
  with the time of day.
- Night `#170C14`: sky and unlit rooms.
- Limewash `#EFE3D2`: text and the reflections.
- Lamplight `#F3B65A`: only light that is really on, and the buttons ("lights on").
- Brick `#A4513A`: the cornice and the stallriser.
- Imbue (variable wght and opsz, OFL): the day name, the headline, the captions.
  It is the bistro Didone, and its weight axis carries data.
- Familjen Grotesk (OFL): body text and UI.

## Word count
**179** visible words on the home page. This excludes the nav, the form labels
and button, the footer, and the Mon–Sun ruler buttons. It includes the day name
on the fascia, the clock, and the SVG tub numbers, so "Monday" is counted twice.
Counted with a parser over `index.html`.

## Illustrative vs. factual
- **Factual:**
  - the morning prep count with a little over
  - blanks with a reason for dishes too new to call
  - the inputs (past sales, day, season, holidays)
  - the nightly read of sales
  - the pilot: free, about five weeks of sales, about a week of setup, a
    nightly download that works with most POS systems, the owner checks the
    counts against what sold and decides
  - privacy
- **Illustrative:**
  - every cover count, tub level and prep number (labelled "Example" on the
    basement window, the payoff and the storyboard)
  - the Friday staffing
  - the Tuesday room

## Live vs. coming
"Lit is Tooey today, reflected is what we're building."
- Prep is drawn as real things in lit rooms: the light catching, the counts on
  the tape, the tubs.
- Growth (the Tuesday room full) and shifts (Friday's two extra cooks) appear
  only as reflections in the glass: mirrored, pale, slightly blurred, under the
  window's sheen.
- The Tuesday caption states the rule once. Friday's caption repeats it ("In the
  glass: the shift we're building").
- There are no badges, dates or roadmap.

## Critique passes
- **Director:**
  - Window frames were black UI borders; they became paint-coloured mouldings.
  - Captions moved from small grotesk to Imbue and were top-aligned, so the
    pier stops reading as an empty sidebar.
  - The basement crop hid the tubs; it moved to a "meet" viewBox with the room
    extended past its edges.
  - The railing in front of the light-well was removed as an accessory, along
    with the glowing button hover and the cornice highlight.
  - The tubs no longer glow in a dark kitchen.
- **Owner:**
  - "Free while we pilot" and "emailed" were added to the hero lead so the first
    five seconds say what it is, what it costs, and how it arrives.
  - Sunday was closing with lights on under a "Lights out" caption. Sunday now
    closes early.
  - The blank dish no longer gets a tick on Thursday, since there is nothing to
    grade.

## Weaknesses
- The reflection device relies on the caption the first time. Pale mirrored
  figures can read as "ghost customers" before the sentence lands.
- Between services the dining window is an empty room for stretches of scroll.
  It is honest, but less eventful mid-afternoon.
- The scene is CPU-drawn SVG with about 60 attribute writes per frame. It was
  smooth in headless Chromium, but this was not tested on a low-end phone.
- Phone landscape (very short viewports) is squeezed. It works, but it was not
  designed for that.
