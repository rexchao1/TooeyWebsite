Privacy

What happens to what you send us, and to your restaurant's sales.

The contact form

When you send the form, your message goes through FormSubmit, a form service, to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use what you send only to reply to you. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Your sales

During the pilot, we set up a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. Tooey uses it to work out your prep counts.

  • Your sales are used only for your restaurant.
  • They are not sold, not shared with other restaurants, and not published.
  • Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting your data

Ask us and we will delete your sales and your messages. Write to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

Who we are

Tooey LLC, a small team of university students. The site has no analytics and sets no tracking cookies.
