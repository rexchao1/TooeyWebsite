# 21 refined

Concept: a plain company site where each animation is light moving over a kitchen material. Prep, which runs today, is solid porcelain in morning light. Shifts and growth, which are being built, are clear smoke glass.

Ground: basalt (#2f2d2a), with porcelain (#f2f1ec), slate (#232a2f, ink on porcelain), ash (#aba69e, secondary text) and one warm light (#f1d2a0). The warm light is used only in the growth lamps.

Type: Commissioner (OFL, self-hosted variable font). Headings use light weights with Flair 40. Body text uses Flair 0.

No libraries. CSS keyframes animate `@property` custom properties. A small `site.js` adds play-on-view, the cursor light, the phone menu and the form.

## Animations
- Hero: the counter starts dark. The day's sales draw in as hairlines. A light front then crosses the stone, the ramekin shadows swing from long to short, and each count comes into focus as the light reaches it. After that, the shadows lean away from the cursor.
- Prep: 24 plates stack, the "Predicted 24" line sets, and then two more plates land with a brighter glaze rim for "Prep 26". The empty place beside the stack is the blank.
- Shifts (concept): a raking light shows a busy night pressed into the stone. Four glass panes then slide in and stack to fit it.
- Growth (concept): on a quiet Tuesday, table lamps come on one by one, and three of those tables get a ring for a regular coming back.

Under reduced motion or without JavaScript, every figure shows its end state.

## Critique passes
- Design director: I removed the hairline rules above the pilot steps, because they repeated what the numbers already say. That was the one accessory taken off. I also softened the ramekin fluting, which first read as bottle caps.
- Owner: "Overnight" alone did not say what the hairlines were, so it became "Overnight, the day's sales". I tightened the hero so the counts sit above the fold at 1440×900.

## Weakness
The meaning of the growth figure (lamps = covers, ring = regular) rests on its caption. Without the caption, it is a mood rather than an explanation.
