Privacy

What we do with what you send us, and with your restaurant's sales.

The contact form

What you type in the form goes through FormSubmit, a form delivery service, to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use it only to reply to you.

Your sales

A restaurant's sales are used only for that restaurant. They are not sold, not shared, and not published.

Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting it

Ask at team@usetooey.com and we delete your messages, your sales, or both.
