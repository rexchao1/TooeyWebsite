Privacy

Tooey LLC is a small team. This page says what we do with what you send us.

The contact form

When you send the form, it goes through FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use what you write only to reply to you. We don't add you to a mailing list.

Your restaurant's sales

During the pilot we set up a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. We use your sales only to make your restaurant's counts.

  • We don't sell them.
  • We don't share them with other restaurants.
  • We don't publish them.
  • Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting your data

Ask and we delete it: your form message, your sales, or both. Write to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

This site

No analytics, no tracking, no cookies. Fonts and scripts are served from this site.
