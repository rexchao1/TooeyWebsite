Privacy

What happens to what you send us, and to your sales.

The contact form

The form sends your name, restaurant, email, and anything else you fill in through FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use it only to reply to you.

Your restaurant’s sales

During the pilot we set up a nightly download of item-level sales from your POS. Those sales are used only for your restaurant. We don’t sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them.

Who sees it

Only the Tooey team.

Deleting it

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and ask. We’ll delete your message, your sales, or both.
