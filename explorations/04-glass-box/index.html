A guessed number would look just as sure as the counts around it, and you’d have no way to tell them apart. Blank doesn’t mean make none: prep those dishes on your own judgment, the way you do now.

Each night, and the morning after

What Tooey does while you’re closed, and what stays with you.

After closeOvernightBefore prepThrough the day

Tooey

  1. After close

    A nightly download picks up the day’s item sales from your POS. Nobody types them in.

  2. Overnight

    Every count is worked out again, with the day just finished added in.

  3. Before prep

    The list arrives in your email, grouped by category.

  4. Through the day

    Nothing until tonight’s sales come in.

You

  1. After close

    Close the way you always do.

  2. Overnight

    Nothing.

  3. Before prep

    Read it, print it, and hand it to whoever is on prep.

  4. Through the day

    Prep from it, adjust for anything Tooey can’t know, and see what actually sells.

The list is a count, not an order. Tooey doesn’t place orders, schedule staff, or change your menu. It hands the kitchen a number for each dish it can call, and the kitchen decides what gets made.

Where Tooey is going

Prep is where Tooey starts, not all it’s for. The aim is software that helps you understand and grow the whole restaurant, from the sales your POS already records, with signals like weather and local events plugged in along the way. Each area would go the same way: what’s likely, then what to do about it, then doing it once you say yes.

Only the filled stop exists today. Every open ring is direction, not a feature.

What it takes to start

What the pilot asks of you, and what we can and can’t tell you yet.

Cost
Free for now, while we’re in a pilot.
Your sales
About five weeks of past sales in your POS. We set up the nightly download, and it works with most systems.
Setup
About a week. You tell us what matters in your kitchen, choose how your list is set up, and check it before you rely on it.
Accuracy
Not measured yet at a live restaurant, so we don’t quote a figure. During the pilot you check the counts against what actually sold, then decide.
Your data
Used only to make your restaurant’s counts. Never sold, shared with other restaurants, or published. Read the privacy page.
