Privacy

When you send the form, your details go through FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use them only to reply to you.

If your restaurant joins the pilot, your sales are used only to forecast for your restaurant. They are not sold, not shared with other restaurants, and not published. Only the Tooey team sees them.

To have your details or your sales deleted, email tooeyteam@gmail.com.

This site uses no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. Tooey LLC.
