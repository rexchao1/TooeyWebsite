Privacy

What reaches us, what it’s used for, and who sees it. Like the prep list, this page also says what we don’t do.

The contact form

When you write to us from this site.

What we get
Your name, restaurant, and email, your phone and POS if you add them, and your message.
How it travels
Through FormSubmit, a form service, to tooeyteam@gmail.com.
Used for
Replying to you.
Never
Sold, or used for ads.

Your sales, if you become a partner

From the nightly download we set up with your POS.

What we get
Item-level sales from your POS.
Used for
Making your restaurant’s counts, and nothing else.
Never
Sold, shared with other restaurants, or published.

Recipes, inventory, and purchasing records

Only if you decide to share them later.

Stays with
Your restaurant.
Used for
Helping with your prep and purchasing, and nothing else.

Who sees it

Only the people on the Tooey team who build your forecast. FormSubmit carries what you send through the contact form on its way to our inbox.

Having it removed

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and say what you want gone. We’ll delete your messages, stop the nightly pull from your POS, or remove the sales we’ve stored.

Questions about any of this? Write to us.
