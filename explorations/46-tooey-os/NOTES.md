# 46 Tooey windows

Round sixteen. Rex has no photos and asked what a website for a computer OS would look like.
The answer: the product is the picture. 46 is 44 with the same words, where the art is Tooey's own windows.
Everything not listed here is 44's: see `../44-typed-os/NOTES.md`.

## What changed
1. **Windows in the hero.** After the headline types, a notification slides in at the top right ("Tooey: Friday's prep sheet is ready.") and a small "Tonight's prep" window rises over the street, with 162 covers expected and three counts.
2. **A desk instead of the room.** "What Tooey does" shows a dotted desk with four Tooey windows laid out the way the data moves: Your POS, Forecast, Prep sheet, Shifts, joined by dotted lines.
   - Pinned, a camera glides (1.15s) from one window to the next as the steps change: forecast, then prep sheet, then shifts. The others dim to half.
   - Each window plays when it comes into focus: forecast bars grow, prep rows rise and tick off and "Ready before prep" stamps, shift bars grow over the busy curve.
   - Unpinned, the whole desk is shown at once and every window has played.
3. **Every sample number is labelled.** Each window carries an "Example" tag, and each figure has an "Example" caption for screen readers.

The street, the independents section, setup and the form are 44's.

## Checked
At 1440×900 in Chromium: the hero at rest with the notification and the window; each of the three pinned steps at rest after the glide.
Phone views were out of scope this round and were not reviewed.

## Weakness
The sample counts are invented; they are labelled Example, but a real restaurant's sheet would be stronger.
The desk is a picture of software, so it says less about the restaurant than the drawn rooms did.
