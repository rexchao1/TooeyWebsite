One free photo, three treatments.

The same Unsplash photo of a prep station, drawn in code three ways, each animated. Last, a scroll demo: the page moves the camera through the photo.

1. Engraved lines

Horizontal slate lines that thicken in the dark, like an engraving or a pen plotter. Draws itself in, then the camera drifts.

2. Typed characters

The photo set in type, row by row, like the headline in 44. A few characters keep resettling while it rests.

3. Warm duotone

Slate to porcelain with a warm middle and live film grain. Develops in, then drifts.
