What we do with what you tell us.

This covers the contact form on this site and, if you join the pilot, your restaurant’s sales. The short version of each part is in the margin.

The contact form

When you write to us from this site, we get your name, your restaurant, your email, your phone number and POS if you add them, and your message. FormSubmit, the service that handles the form, passes it to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use it to reply to you. We don’t sell it, and we don’t use it for ads.

Used to reply to you, and nothing else.

Your sales, if you join the pilot

Setup includes a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. We use those sales to make the forecast for your restaurant, and for nothing else. We don’t sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them.

Your sales count for your restaurant only.

Recipes, inventory and purchasing

If you later share recipes, inventory or purchasing records with us, they stay with your restaurant. We use them only to help with your prep and purchasing.

Only if you choose to share them.

Who sees it

Only the people on the Tooey team who build your forecast. FormSubmit handles messages from the contact form on their way to us.

A small team, and only the people doing the work.

Getting it removed

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com and ask us to delete your messages, stop the nightly download, or remove the sales we’ve stored.

One email does it.

Questions about any of this are welcome. Write to us.
