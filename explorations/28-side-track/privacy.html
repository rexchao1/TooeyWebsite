Privacy

What we do with what you send us, and with your restaurant's sales.

The contact form

What you type in the form goes through FormSubmit, a form delivery service, to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use it only to reply to you.

Your sales

A restaurant's sales are used only to make that restaurant's counts. They are not sold, not shared with other restaurants, and not published.

Only the Tooey team sees them.

Deleting it

Ask at tooeyteam@gmail.com and we delete your messages, your sales, or both.

This site

No analytics and no cookies. Fonts are served from this site.
