What happens to what you send us

What we collect, what it’s for, who sees it, and how to have it removed.

When you write to us

The form on this site sends your name, your restaurant, your email, your phone number and POS if you add them, and your message. It goes through a service called FormSubmit to tooeyteam@gmail.com.

We use it to reply to you. We don’t sell it, and we don’t use it for ads.

If you join the pilot

During setup we arrange a nightly download of your item-level sales from your POS. Those sales are used to forecast for your restaurant and for nothing else.

We don’t sell them, share them with other restaurants, or publish them.

Recipes, inventory, and purchasing

If you later share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records with us, they stay with your restaurant. We use them only to help with your prep and purchasing.

Who sees it

Only the people on the Tooey team who build your forecast. FormSubmit carries contact form messages to our inbox.

Having it removed

Email tooeyteam@gmail.com. You can ask us to delete your messages, stop the nightly download, or remove the sales we’ve stored, and we’ll do it.

This website

No analytics, no tracking, and no cookies. The fonts are served from this site, so loading a page doesn’t tell anyone else you visited.

Anything else

If something about your data isn’t answered here, write to us and ask.
