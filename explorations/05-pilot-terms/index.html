What arrives each morning

One list, by email, before prep starts, with dishes grouped the way your menu is. Print it and keep it on the line.

Predicted
How many of that dish we expect you to sell today.
Prep
How many to make. A little over Predicted, so a rush doesn’t leave the line short.
vs. last Tue
The change from the same day last week, so a big swing shows up before service.
A blank
Under about five weeks on the menu, or under about one sold a day. You get the reason instead of a guess. A blank is a decline, not a zero.

Every count is worked out again each night from your own sales, the day of the week, the time of year, and holidays. Not yet weather, local events, or reservations. You know about the game in town or the storm on the way and the list doesn’t, which is why the call stays yours.

The terms

What you’d put in, what you’d get back, and what it costs. If a term isn’t written here, it hasn’t been decided yet. Ask, and we’ll tell you where it stands.

You put in You get back

  1. To begin

    You put in
    A message about your restaurant and which POS you use.
    You get back
    A reply from the team, and a straight answer on whether we can pull your sales. Square, Toast, and Clover sales exports are already recognized. Exports from other systems can be mapped.

  2. Setup about a week

    You put in
    Access to your POS sales, with about five weeks of past sales to start from. Time to tell us what matters in your kitchen, choose how your list is laid out, and check it before you rely on it.
    You get back
    A nightly download from your POS, which we set up. A list laid out the way you chose.

  3. Every morning after

    You put in
    A look at our counts next to what actually sold, then your own call on what to prep. When a count looks off, tell us.
    You get back
    A count for every dish in your inbox before prep, worked out again each night from the latest sales.

  4. If you stop

    You put in
    One email to tooeyteam@gmail.com.
    You get back
    We stop the nightly download. If you ask, we also delete the sales we’ve stored and the messages you’ve sent.

Prep is the first part of Tooey

We want Tooey to be where an independent owner goes to see how the restaurant is really doing and what to change: starting from what your POS already knows, then suggesting what to do, and later doing some of that work for you. We started with prep because your kitchen makes that call every day.

Restaurants in the pilot are the kitchens we’ll learn from when we decide what to build next.

  • Prep countsIn the pilot now
  • ProfitabilityWhich dishes make money, where margins slipNot built
  • GrowthNew guests, and regulars coming backNot built
  • OperationsLess waste, simpler daysNot built
  • Better forecastsWeather, local events, other signalsNot built
  • StaffingLabor planned around expected demandNot built
  • Your numbers togetherTurned into decisions you can act onNot built

None of these exist yet, and the order may change. They’re here so you know where this is headed.
