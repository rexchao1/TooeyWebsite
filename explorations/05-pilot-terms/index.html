What arrives each morning

One list, by email, before prep starts. Every dish on your menu with the numbers you need to decide how much to make. Print it and keep it on the line.

Predicted
How many of that dish we expect you to sell today.
Prep
How many to make. It’s a little over Predicted, so a rush doesn’t leave the line short.
vs. last Tue
The change from the same day last week. A big swing is easier to deal with before service than in the middle of it.
A blank
When a dish has been on the menu less than about five weeks, or sells less than about one a day, we leave it blank and give the reason. We’d rather tell you we don’t know than hand you a guess.
Categories
Dishes are grouped by category, the way your menu already is, so each station can find its part.

What goes into a count

Each night, after the day’s sales come in, every count is worked out again from four things:

  • Your own sales, dish by dish
  • The day of the week
  • The time of year
  • Holidays

Not yet weather, local events, or reservations. If there’s a game in town or a storm on the way, you know it and the list doesn’t. That’s one reason the counts are yours to check, and the call is yours to make.

The terms

What you’d put in, what you’d get back, and what it costs. If a term isn’t written here, it hasn’t been decided yet. Ask, and we’ll tell you where it stands.

You put in You get back

  1. To begin

    You put in
    A message about your restaurant and which POS you use.
    You get back
    A reply from the team, and a straight answer on whether we can pull your sales. Square, Toast, and Clover sales exports are already recognized. Exports from other systems can be mapped.

  2. Setup about a week

    You put in
    Access to your POS sales, with about five weeks of past sales to start from. Time to tell us what matters in your kitchen, choose how your list is laid out, and check it before you rely on it.
    You get back
    A nightly download from your POS, which we set up. A list laid out the way you chose.

  3. Every morning after

    You put in
    A look at our counts next to what actually sold, then your own call on what to prep. When a count looks off, tell us.
    You get back
    A count for every dish in your inbox before prep, worked out again each night from the latest sales.

  4. If you stop

    You put in
    One email to tooeyteam@gmail.com.
    You get back
    We stop the nightly download. If you ask, we also delete the sales we’ve stored and the messages you’ve sent.

Prep is the first part of Tooey

The aim is bigger than a prep list. We want Tooey to be where an independent owner goes to see how the restaurant is really doing and what to change: starting from what your POS already knows, then suggesting what to do about it, and later doing some of that work for you.

We started with prep because it’s a call your kitchen makes every day, and your sales already say a lot about it.

The restaurants in the pilot are the kitchens we’ll be learning from when we decide what to build next. Joining now means yours is one of them.

  • Prep counts A count for every dish, each morning. In the pilot now
  • Profitability Which dishes actually make money, where margins slip, and which costs you could do without. Not built yet
  • Growth Bringing new guests in and getting regulars back more often. Not built yet
  • Operations Less waste and simpler days, past the prep list. Not built yet
  • Better forecasts Counts that also weigh weather, local events, and other signals. Not built yet
  • Staffing Labor planned around how busy you’re likely to be. Not built yet
  • Your numbers together Sales and the rest in one place, turned into decisions you can act on. Not built yet

None of these exist yet, and we may get to them in a different order. They’re here so you know where this is headed, not to sell you on them.
