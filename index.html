What Tooey does

Three core operations, planned daily from your own sales data.

  1. Forecast

    How busy each day will be and what will sell, worked out from your own sales.

  2. Prep

    How much of each dish to make, ready every morning before prep: a little over what should sell.

  3. Inventory

    What's on the shelf against what the next few days will use, and what to order before the cutoff.

Built for independent restaurants.

Large chains have entire teams running their back office. Independent restaurants have the owner. Tooey is that team.
