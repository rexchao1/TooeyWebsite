Every morning

Most prep starts with a guess.

Someone looks at yesterday's tickets and picks a number. Guess high and the extra goes in the bin. Guess low and you 86 a dish in the middle of service.

Chains pay a team to forecast this. Independent kitchens get yesterday's tickets.

Most kitchens waste 4 to 10 percent of the food they buy.LeanPath, from kitchens that weigh their waste.

How a day goes

It runs while you close.

  1. After close1

  2. Tonight2

    A number for each dish, and a range.

  3. Morning3

    Print it. Tape it up.

    Hang it where you prep, and prep to the number.

    Tell us where it was off.
