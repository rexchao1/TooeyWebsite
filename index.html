Talk to us How it works

After a week of onboarding, you get tomorrow's list every night.

Tooey pulls from your POS every night and emails a per-item forecast for the next day. You print the list, then decide if the numbers are any good.

  1. Onboarding takes about a week.

    We connect to the POS you already use and set up a collector that pulls sales every night. You do not send us a spreadsheet to get started.

  2. Tomorrow's forecast is emailed every night.

    Each item gets a count for the next day. You print the email and hang it where you prep.

  3. You decide if the numbers are any good.

    Compare the forecast to what actually sold for a week, or we can backtest on your history. You decide whether to keep going.

  4. Inventory and purchasing come later.

    The first job is the per-item count, so prep is tighter and there is less waste. Inventory and more efficient purchasing come next.
