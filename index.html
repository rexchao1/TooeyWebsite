Talk to us How it works

The forecast has to be useful first.

We start with sales. You keep running the kitchen the way you already do. After a week, you decide if the numbers are close enough to go further.

  1. Send sales history.

    Item-level, from the POS you already have. A spreadsheet is fine. Covers help if you keep them.

  2. Tooey forecasts a week.

    You don't change how you prep or buy. You just look at a count next to each dish.

  3. Compare it to what sold.

    If the counts are close enough, you say so. If they aren't, we don't go further.

  4. Prep and purchasing come after.

    Recipes, inventory, vendors. Optional, and only once the forecast has earned it.
