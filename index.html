The Tooey trust process

We earn your trust before asking for more.

You shouldn't have to hand over everything on day one. Tooey is designed to prove its value first.

01

Start with sales

Give Tooey access to historical item-level sales and relevant information such as covers. We start with one question: Can Tooey accurately predict what your restaurant will sell?

02

One week, no commitment

For the first week, Tooey simply forecasts how many of each menu item we believe you will sell. You don't have to change your preparation or purchasing process. Compare our forecast with your actual sales.

03

You decide if we're accurate enough

If you're happy with Tooey's accuracy — or think it outperforms your current forecasting process — you can choose whether to take the next step. The restaurant decides when Tooey has earned its trust.

04

Unlock prep & inventory optimization

Once you're comfortable with the forecast, you can provide recipes, ingredient quantities, inventory, purchasing, storage, and other operational information. Tooey can then translate expected item sales into practical recommendations.

First, prove the forecast. Then earn the opportunity to help with the rest.
