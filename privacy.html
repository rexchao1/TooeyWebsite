Privacy

What the form sends, and what happens if we work together.

The contact form

Name, restaurant, email, phone if you add it, and your message. Submitting the form emails tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use it to write back. We don't sell it, and we don't put it in an ad.

If we work together

Sales history is used to forecast for your restaurant. Recipes, inventory, and purchasing, if you share them later, are used to turn that forecast into prep and purchasing help. That's the job, not a dataset for someone else.
