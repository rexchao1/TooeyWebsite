Privacy

This page covers what we collect, why we collect it, and who sees it.

The contact form

When you write us from the site, we receive your name, restaurant, email, phone if you add it, and your message. FormSubmit sends that to tooeyteam@gmail.com. We use it to reply. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for ads.

If you become a restaurant partner

Onboarding includes connecting to your POS so an automatic collector can pull item-level sales every night. That history is used to forecast for your restaurant, and nowhere else. The nightly email we send you is built from that pull. We do not sell sales data, we do not share it with other restaurants, and we do not turn it into a public dataset.

If you later share recipes, inventory, or purchasing records, those stay attached to your restaurant and are used only to turn the forecast into prep and purchasing help.

Who can see it

The people on the Tooey team who are building your forecast and answering your email. FormSubmit processes the contact form. Your POS vendor already holds the underlying sales. We do not send that data anywhere else.

If you want data removed

Write tooeyteam@gmail.com. Tell us to delete the contact note, stop the nightly collector, or remove sales we have stored for your restaurant, and we will.
