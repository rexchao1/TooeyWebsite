# Site redesign

Goal: replace the generic cream-and-serif look with one a restaurant owner
recognizes as theirs, and cut the copy down to short, plain lines.

Direction (chosen in review): back of house, plain, and honest about being
early. The printed prep list taped to the wall is the main visual.

Colors: receipt paper `#F2F3F0`, printer ink `#1B1B1D`, pencil `#5B5D61`,
steel line `#D3D5D0`, ribbon red `#C8202F`. Red marks one thing per screen,
never a button. Weekday colors (the food-rotation day-dot code: Mon blue, Tue
yellow, Wed red, Thu brown, Fri green, Sat orange, Sun black) appear only as
small dots next to a day's name.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, Instrument Sans for text, IBM Plex
Mono for the list and small labels. All self-hosted in `fonts/`.

Constraints: no invented figures, no phone number until the team gives one,
keep the FormSubmit contact form and its field names, keep the phone bottom
bar and menu sheet.

Done when: all four pages are rebuilt, read well at desktop and phone width,
and the pull request is open.
