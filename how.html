How it works

Set up once.
A list every night.

Setup takes about a week. After that it runs on its own.

Talk to us

  1. Week one

    We connect to your POS.

    We hook up the POS you already use and turn on a nightly pull of your item sales. You don't send us spreadsheets.

    Setup
    • Connect your POS
    • Turn on the nightly pull
    • Pick who gets the email

  2. Every night

    Tomorrow's list is in your email.

    A count for each dish for the next day. Print it and prep from it, or open it in the app.

    Why a range? No forecast is exact. The range shows how sure it is. It's a starting point. You still make the call.
