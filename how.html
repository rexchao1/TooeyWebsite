How Tooey works

You spend a week onboarding. After that, you get a forecast for tomorrow every night, and you decide if the numbers are any good.

  1. Onboarding takes about a week.

    We sit with you, connect to the POS you already run, and set up an automatic collector. After that, sales pull every night. You do not start by emailing us a spreadsheet.

  2. Tomorrow's forecast is emailed every night.

    After the nightly POS pull, you get an email with a count for each item for the next day.

  3. You print the list.

    It is a list for the kitchen. You hang it, prep from the number next to each dish, and keep running service the way you already do.

  4. You decide if the numbers are any good.

    You can compare each night's forecast to what actually sold, for about a week. We can also backtest on your history. You decide whether the counts are close enough to keep using.

What we need from you

  • Time to onboard, about a week.
  • Access to the POS so the nightly collector can run.
  • Feedback once the emails start: what looks right, what does not.

What you get

  • A per-item forecast every night, in email.
  • A list you can print, so nobody is guessing the prep.
  • Tighter prep and less waste, which is how food cost and margin get better.

What comes later

Inventory is next. The same nightly forecast can point at what you buy and hold, so purchasing is as efficient as prep. That is not the first step. The first step is the count for each dish.
