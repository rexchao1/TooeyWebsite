How the counts are made.

What goes into each count, and how to read the list you get each morning.

What goes in

  • Your sales
  • Day of the week
  • Time of year
  • Holidays

Reading the list

  1. Predicted. How many we expect you to sell.
  2. Prep. How many to make. A little over Predicted, so a rush doesn't leave you short.
  3. vs. last Tue. Against the same day last week, so a big change stands out.
