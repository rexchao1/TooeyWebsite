How it works

Set up once.
A list every night.

Setup takes about a week. After that it runs on its own, every night after close.

Talk to us

  1. Week one

    We connect to your POS.

    We sit down with you, hook up the POS you already run, and turn on a nightly pull of your sales. You don't send us spreadsheets.

    Setup

    • Connect the POS you already use
    • Turn on the nightly sales pull
    • Choose who gets the email

  2. Every night

    Tomorrow's list lands in your inbox.

    After the nightly pull, you get an email with a count for each dish for the next day. Print it and hang it where you prep.

    Why a range? No forecast is exact. The range shows how sure it is, so you can decide how much cushion to prep.

  3. The first week

    You check it against real sales.

    For about a week, compare each night's list with what actually sold. We can also test it on your past sales. You decide if it's close enough to keep.

What we need from you

  • About a week for setup.

What you get
