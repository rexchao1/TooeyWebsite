We're building it with people who still have to guess the prep.

Someone is staring at yesterday's tickets, trying to guess tomorrow's prep. Over-prep hits food cost. Under-prep hits the ticket. Owners already know that. They still have to guess, because the POS will not help. It already happened.

Tooey is a forecast from your own sales history. How many of each item, not a total for the day. Built around your restaurant.

We start there on purpose. Recipes, inventory, and purchasing can come later, if the counts are good enough that you want them to. The first week is just the forecast, sitting next to the way you already work.

We're a small team. Tooey is free for the restaurants we work with, for a little while. We'll answer the email.
