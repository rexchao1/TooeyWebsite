We are university students who saw a kitchen problem.

We saw restaurants guessing tomorrow's prep, and a chance to cut food waste and run the line more efficiently.

Tooey was started by university students. The pattern we kept seeing was the same: someone staring at yesterday's tickets, trying to guess how much to prep. Over-prep hits food cost. Under-prep hits the ticket. The POS already recorded what happened. It does not tell you what to cut in the morning.

Tooey is our answer: item-level forecasts from each restaurant's own history, emailed every night and printed for the kitchen. Each dish gets a number instead of a guess, so prep is tighter, waste is lower, and the same food you already buy is used more efficiently.

We are a small team, and we stay reachable. During onboarding and after, you write tooeyteam@gmail.com and a person writes back. If a night's email is late, a number looks off, or the collector misses a pull, we treat that as our problem to fix, not yours to diagnose.

Support is part of the product. We would rather hear from you twice in a week than have a forecast sitting unused because nobody answered.
