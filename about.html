About

We kept seeing kitchens guess.

Tooey is a small team of university students building for independent restaurants.

In restaurant after restaurant, someone looked at yesterday's tickets every morning and guessed how much to prep.

Guess high and it hits food cost. Guess low and a dish runs out in the middle of service. Chains have forecasting teams for this. Independent kitchens don't.

The POS already had the answer in it. It just never said how much to make tomorrow. So Tooey reads your own sales and sends a number for each dish, every night.

We pick up

We're small, and we answer. Write tooeyteam@gmail.com and one of us writes back. If an email is late or a number looks wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

We'd rather hear from you twice a week than have a list sitting unused.
