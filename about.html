A small team, a kitchen problem.

Tooey was started by a group of students. We're building it with a couple of restaurants.

There's an afternoon that keeps repeating. Someone is staring at yesterday's tickets, trying to guess tomorrow's prep. Over-prep hits food cost. Under-prep hits the ticket. Owners already know that. They still have to guess, because the POS will not help. It already happened.

Tooey is a forecast from your own sales history. How many of each item, not a total for the day. Built around your restaurant. Not a generic model.

We start there on purpose. Recipes, inventory, and purchasing can come later, if the counts are good enough that you want them to. The first week is just the forecast, sitting next to the way you already work.

We're a small team. Tooey is free for the restaurants we work with, for a little while, while we learn from real service. We will answer the email.

If you want to try a week, write us.
